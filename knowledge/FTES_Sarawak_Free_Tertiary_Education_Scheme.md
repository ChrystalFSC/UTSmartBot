Sarawak Free Tertiary Education Scheme (FTES)

FTES stands for the Sarawak Free Tertiary Education Scheme. It is a Sarawak State Government scheme, announced in the Sarawak Budget 2023 by Premier Datuk Patinggi Tan Sri Abang Johari Tun Openg, that gives eligible Sarawakians free tuition for a first undergraduate degree at Sarawak-owned institutions of higher learning (SIHL). Implementation began with the 2026 intake.

Participating institutions
University of Technology Sarawak (UTS), Swinburne University of Technology Sarawak Campus, Curtin University Malaysia and i-CATS. As of February 2026, 5,784 students had benefited from FTES, with UTS having the most: 2,991 students (Swinburne Sarawak 2,009, Curtin Malaysia 573, i-CATS 211). (The Borneo Post, 13 February 2026)

Eligibility
- Sarawakian
- Studying for a first bachelor's degree
- Offered a place in an FTES-approved undergraduate degree programme at a Sarawak-owned institution such as UTS
- Full-time student
- Not receiving any other scholarship
- Eligible fields: STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics), Medicine, Law, Psychology, Accounting and Finance
FTES programmes at UTS (from the UTS tuition fee schedule, https://www.uts.edu.my/tuitionfees, where the Sarawak fee for these is RM0):
- School of Engineering and Technology: Bachelor of Civil Engineering, Bachelor of Mechanical Engineering, Bachelor of Electrical Engineering, Bachelor of Food Technology, Bachelor of Science in Occupational Safety and Health, Bachelor of Marine Engineering Technology
- School of Business and Management: Bachelor of Accountancy, Bachelor of Technology Management
- School of Built Environment: Bachelor of Quantity Surveying, Bachelor of Science in Architecture, Bachelor of Arts in Interior Design, Bachelor of Science in Property and Construction Management
- School of Computing and Creative Media: Bachelor of Computer Science, Bachelor of Arts in Industrial Design, Bachelor of Arts in Creative Digital Media, Bachelor of Mobile Game Development
Not FTES programmes: Bachelor of Business Administration and Bachelor of Business in Marketing (Sarawakians get the 50% UTS Bursary Scheme on these instead).
Students from West Malaysia and Sabah are not covered by FTES; they get a 30% UTS Bursary Scheme on FTES programmes and 25% on the other two.

What FTES covers
- Tuition fee waiver of 100% for the period stated in the offer letter
- Stipend of RM15,000 per year for students from households with a monthly per capita income of RM1,500 or less: RM5,000 for accommodation through the designated SIHL housing unit and RM10,000 paid through SPay Global (SarawakPay)
- Not covered: study extensions, repeated or failed subjects, and programme-change fees

Rules
- Applications, including for the stipend, are made through the institution (UTS) as part of the university application.
- One programme change is allowed within the same institution, with Ministry approval. Transfers between institutions are not allowed; students from another institution must apply again and start from Year 1.
- Deferment of up to 2 semesters is allowed for valid reasons; FTES is suspended during deferment.
- FTES, including the stipend, is suspended or ended if the student is suspended, terminated or withdraws from the university.

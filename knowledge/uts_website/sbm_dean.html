FROM THE DEAN'S DESK

A word from your Dean.

Welcome to the School of Business and Management. It is my honour to lead a School that sits at an exciting and demanding moment in the story of business education. The world our graduates are entering is changing faster than at any point in living memory. Artificial intelligence, digital platforms, and new technologies are reshaping how businesses operate, how decisions are made, and how value is created.

Dr. Rahmat Aidil Bin Djubair Dean, School of Business and Management

// VISION & MISSION

A global technical university — built on science and technology.

We are transforming the way we teach. Across our undergraduate programmes, we are embedding artificial intelligence, digitalization, and technology into every subject, not as an add on, but as a natural part of how modern business is understood and practised. Our students will not simply learn about the digital economy. They will learn to work within it, with the tools, the platforms, and the AI capabilities that today's employers expect.

// PROGRAMMES UNDER THE DEAN

What we offer.

This hands on philosophy extends to our research. We are shifting our research culture away from theory for its own sake and towards work that solves real problems for real industries. Our research now focuses strongly on artificial intelligence, digital transformation, and technology, applied directly to the needs of businesses and communities in Sarawak and beyond. Underpinning all of this is our commitment to our people. Our academic staff are continuously upskilling in AI, digital tools, and applied practice, because we cannot teach what we do not live ourselves.

// OUR COMMITMENT

Theory, practice, and the people who guide both.

To our students, current and future, I make this promise. You will leave SBM not only with a qualification, but with real, demonstrable, future ready skills and the confidence to apply them. To our industry partners, I extend an open invitation to build with us, to bring us your real challenges and to help shape graduates who are ready to contribute from day one. The School of Business and Management is not standing still. We are moving, deliberately and boldly, towards a future defined by technology, practical capability, and genuine relevance. I warmly invite you to be part of that journey with us.

Thank you.

Dr. Rahmat Aidil Bin Djubair

Dean, School of Business and Management
